GLM-5.3-Flash on 2x RTX PRO 6000: results

Accuracy, completion, speed, KV capacity and kernel tests of GLM-5.3-Flash EXL3 quants on tpurtell's vLLM-based engine, under fixed, versioned protocols. Every number links to its receipts. What else can move a result: Confounds. What changed and when, including one retraction: Ledger.

What the results say

  1. No tested configuration is measurably more accurate on GPQA Diamond. Three-pass records: 85.7-88.2%; pass 1 of all 10 configurations: 84.3-87.9%. Descriptive tables
  2. One pass varies by 0.5-3.5 points between passes with their own seeds: as much as configurations differ. Descriptive chart
  3. tpurtell 0.7.0 as shipped left fewer questions unanswered than 0.9.1: 4 vs 16 empty answers of 594 (3.25bpw). The cause is open. Supported comparisons · confounds
  4. 3.25bpw and 4bpw TR3 (Brandon) perform alike on 0.9.1, in accuracy and in completion. 4bpw's KV pool holds about 4 requests at the token cap. Descriptive records · KV
  5. tpurtell's DCP1 layout with MLA layer ownership holds the most KV cache: 4,707,515 tokens for 3.25bpw on 0.9.1. Supported KV table
  6. Neither engine fix costs speed, in single runs. Descriptive speed
  7. Engine issues found during evaluation are fixed in current releases: two upstream kpool issues (0.9.0) and the DCP1 tail masking issue (0.9.1). The 0.9.0 and 0.9.1 images pass 33 of 33 upstream kernel tests. Supported issues and fixes

Scoring and KV notes

The raw filter reads some correct answers as wrong; the stated-answer score is 0.5-2.0 points higher per pass-1 run (scoring). 4bpw TR3 (Brandon) leaves a KV pool of about 1.38 million tokens, about 4 requests at the token cap: its 0.9.1 record runs 4 at once, and its pass-1 runs at 8 waited for KV at times (logged on 0.9.1; 0.8.0 and 0.9.0 kept no server log). No higher-precision version of the model was run on this hardware.
