What it shows

  1. Accuracy does not depend on engine, patches, draft depth or quantization here: every configuration lands at 85-88%.
  2. Finishing long reasoning depends on the engine. On the same weights, 0.7.0 leaves about a third as many answers empty as 0.8.0.
  3. The two kpool bugs were real and are fixed in 0.9.0, at no measurable cost, but they are not the cause of the loops. Their benefit is in long-lived servers with prefix caching, which these fresh-server runs rarely exercise.
  4. The gap to published scores (NVIDIA 92.1, Red Hat 90.6) is not a runtime problem: removing every empty answer would add 1-3 points.
