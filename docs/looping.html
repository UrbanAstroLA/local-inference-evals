The DCP1 tail issue and its fix

What happened. Each decode step attends up to 2,048 earlier tokens, picked in pools of 4. The newest pool is incomplete: the current token and up to two before it (the kpool tail). In the DCP1 layout its columns were masked out at causal lengths up to 2,043 not divisible by 4, so every MLA layer missed those tokens.

Design context. DCP1 with MLA layer ownership is tpurtell's layout from 0.8.0 on. It holds about 1.5x the KV cache of v0.7.0's DCP2 layout (4,707,515 vs 3,165,056 tokens on the same image). The masking path already existed in the vendored attention code; only this layout exercises it.

The fix, tpurtell PR #6, compacts the valid entries before the mask, in the DCP1 branch only. Merged 2026-10-08; released in tpurtell 0.9.1.

Mechanism in detail
  • The indexer writes the selected pools to columns 0-2043 and the tail to the fixed columns 2044-2046.
  • In the DCP1 branch the selection length becomes min(causal length, 2,048); every column at or beyond it is masked.
  • Not affected: DCP2, which compacts its selection, and the dense short-prefill path.
  • Measured on a local build of the fix before the merge (tpurtell 0.9.0 + DCP1 tail fix ≈ 0.9.1); the decode-vs-prefill repeat ran on the 0.9.1 release.

Non-completion: what the clean data shows

Component screen, 2026-10-09, preregistered: one question per arm, 12 draws with distinct request seeds.

Scope, power and what was held fixed
Scope of the component screen.
Held fixed. In every arm: 3.25bpw weights, DFlash2 ×3, temperature 1.0 / top_p 0.95, the 327,680-token budget, 12 concurrent requests, one question per arm, a fresh server.
Not separated. Arms EO and NO also turned draft-slot sharing off (the launcher pairs it with MLA ownership tp), so ownership and sharing are not separated. Arm V79 differs from B79 in image, layout and vision together.
Not varied. Draft depth, quantization and sampling settings.
Power. Two-sided Fisher test, p < 0.05, 80% power (tools/analyze.py screen-power): one arm against another on question 88 (12 vs 12, from 25% failing) detects a rise of about 60 points, and a drop is not reachable at all; a switch on vs off (24 vs 24, from 17%) detects a rise of about 41 points; question 79 (12 vs 12, from 75%) detects a drop of about 60 points. Only very large effects could show.
Selection. Questions 88 and 79 were chosen as hard from empty answers and screens that all sent request seed 1234; with distinct seeds, question 88 fails in 1-3 of 12 draws per arm. Two questions are not a benchmark-wide rate.

How the failures look

Question 88 fails by looping; question 79 mostly by exhaustion. No finished or exhausted request was stopped by the early-stop detector.

Across GPQA: empty answers in three passes

tpurtell 0.7.0 as shipped left 4 of 594 answers empty, 0.9.1 left 16 (3.25bpw; supported). Accuracy does not differ measurably (clustered p = 0.41). The two differ in several ways at once, so the cause is open (confounds). Tables and the per-question grid: Results.

Seeds and the earlier screens

The earlier screens sent seed 1234 on every repeat. Their rates were withdrawn on 2026-10-09 and replaced by the component screen above (ledger; why: confounds).
