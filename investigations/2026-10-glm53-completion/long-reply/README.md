# Long-reply completion (amendments 22q-22al; closed)

The question: what drives completion of GPQA replies that run past 100,000 generated tokens?

On the old records, P(finish | still generating at 100k) was 0.71 for stock 0.7.0 and 0.40 for stock 0.9.1. The
fresh-seed replication of stock 0.7.0 gave 0.66. All of these are descriptive, with about 10-19 finishing events per
record.

**Non-completion is not the same as looping. Both are measured here, not assumed.** "Finishing" in the continuation
experiments means the frozen stop-token endpoint within +65,536 tokens; a valid final answer is checked separately
(amendment 22ai).

## 1. Discovery (zero GPU; `discovery/`)
Reasoning text from the stock 0.7.0 replication, with definitions hashed before any metric was computed (`PRESPEC.md`):

- **Capped replies:** 8 of 10 end in an exact loop (period 2-1,001 tokens). Four are 2-token loops: an interjection
  plus a separator. 2 replies do not loop.
- **Onsets:** only 2 were already looping at 100k; 6 began 43k-188k tokens later and 2 never formed a terminal loop (erratum 22am). No capped reply recovered from
  a terminal loop.
- **Finished replies:** 15 of 19 produce new text to the end of their reasoning.
- **Early signatures:** capped replies had lower characters per token in their first 100k tokens (2.31 against 3.15)
  and more interjections, before any repetition.
- **Geometry:** onset positions do not cluster on the attention block, prefill chunk or powers of two.
- **Question-matched:** on the 14 questions long on both engine families, the 0.7.0 family finished 23 / 36 long
  replies and the 0.9.1 family 18 / 58. The 0.9.1 family pools stock 0.9.1 with the modified col511, B0 and B1
  arms, and seeds 1234-1236 are shared across engines (caveat restored, 22an). Which questions go long does not
  explain the gap.

## 2. B+: fixed 0.7.0-built prefixes, continued on three engines (`bplus/`; 13.06 GPU-hours)
- **Design:** 12 prefixes cut at 100k from 0.7.0 replies, 4 seeds each, +64k tokens on stock 0.7.0, stock 0.9.1 and B1.
- **Validity:** the frozen analysis was INVALID on an over-strict KV-pool gate (a size the 0.7.0 record had itself
  produced). The gate was amended before any outcome was read (22v), and the result is reported as read after that
  disclosed amendment.
- **Reading: R-I, inconclusive.**

| engine | P(finish within +64k) |
|---|---:|
| stock 0.7.0 | 0.417 |
| stock 0.9.1 | 0.417 |
| B1 | 0.521 |

- **0.7.0 − 0.9.1:** 0.000 (95% −0.083 to +0.083).
- **Between-prefix variation:** η² 0.67 in the observed sample. This is not a causal share.
- **Why not "state-dominated":** the B1 contrasts missed the |mean| ≤ 0.10 rule by 0.004.
- **What it shows:** under a disclosed, outcome-blind amendment to the KV-capacity gate, both stock engines stopped
  within the continuation budget on 20/48 requests. The estimated difference was 0.000, with a prefix-bootstrap 95%
  interval of −0.083 to +0.083. The overall reading remained R-I. The prefixes are token prefixes generated by
  0.7.0, each reconstructed by every engine's prefill, so this is an effect of the whole engine bundle (prefill
  reconstruction plus continuation). It is not an effect from identical internal states.
- **Limits:**
  - one 0.7.0-built state per question;
  - prefixes rebuilt by prefill, so any decode-built state is absent;
  - re-tokenized ids;
  - "engine" bundles decode path, defect exposure, layout and speculation depth.

## 3. Option D: one stock 0.9.1 pass with full text (`d091/`; 5.88 GPU-hours)
- **The pass:** valid. 6 of 198 empty, inside the record's range; stated accuracy 88.4%.
- **P(finish | 100k):** 4 / 10 (0.40), the same as the 0.9.1 record.
- **Capped replies:** all 6 end in short-period exact loops, the same kind as 0.7.0's.
- **Early signatures:**
  - the low characters-per-token signature is weak on 0.9.1 (2.97 against 3.27);
  - the interjection signature holds (capped 9.4 against finished 2.7 per 1k tokens).
- **Prefixes:** 8 0.9.1-built prefixes exported for a cross-continuation (option E).
- **Limits:** descriptive only. One pass, 10 replies at risk, seeds different from every comparator.

## 4. Option D2: a second stock 0.9.1 pass with full text (`d091b/`; 7.16 GPU-hours)
- **The pass:** valid. 8 of 198 empty (D: 6; record: 5-6 per pass); stated accuracy 85.4%.
- **P(finish | 100k):** 4 / 12 (0.33).
- **Capped replies:** 7 of 8 end in exact loops; none was repeating at 100k.
- **First 100k tokens, capped against finished:**
  - characters per token 2.68 against 3.37;
  - digit share 0.072 against 0.030;
  - interjections 11.6 against 2.3 per 1k.

  The digit-heavy early signature appears here, unlike in D.
- **Prefixes:** D2 adds 5 prefixes on new questions.

## 5. Option E: 0.9.1-generated prefixes on both engines (`e-cross/`; 10.24 GPU-hours)
- **Design:** 13 token prefixes generated by 0.9.1 (8 from D, 5 from D2; 8 originally capped, 5 finished), each
  reconstructed by each engine's prefill. 4 seeds each, +64k tokens on stock 0.7.0 and stock 0.9.1, under B+'s design
  and screens, interpreted per amendment 22ai.
- **Validity:** valid; 104 / 104 requests OK. **Reading: R-I.**
- **Stop-token endpoint within +64k:**

  | engine | P | stopped / 52 |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | stock 0.7.0 | 0.423 | 22 |
  | stock 0.9.1 | 0.288 | 15 |

- **E1 = +0.135** (95% +0.038 to +0.250; two-sided sign-flip p 0.0625, which is the test's floor with 5 discordant
  prefixes). The point estimate is below the 0.15 EFFECT threshold; not SMALL.
- **Between-prefix variation:** η² 0.72 in the observed sample.
- **Cross (descriptive):** 0.7.0-generated prefixes 0.417 / 0.417, 0.9.1-generated prefixes 0.423 / 0.288 (v070 /
  v091). Interaction +0.135 (95% +0.013 to +0.271; permutation p 0.10). The source contrast is confounded with
  question.
- **Answer audit (descriptive):** every stopped continuation gave a final letter (22 / 22 and 15 / 15). Correct:
  12 / 22 and 10 / 15.

## Status at close (amendment 22al)

In brief:
- In B+, the stock engines stopped at identical aggregate rates on selected 0.7.0-generated prefixes.
- In E, 0.7.0 stopped more often on selected 0.9.1-generated prefixes (+13.5 points; 5 prefixes favouring 0.7.0, none
  favouring 0.9.1).
- Neither met its frozen criterion. The pattern suggests possible dependence on prefix source; it does not establish
  an interaction or locate its cause.
- Additional stops did not translate into comparably more correct answers: 23.1% against 19.2% correct over all
  attempts.
